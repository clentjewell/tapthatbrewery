**Unmarketed product truth:** the range is gluten-reduced (cannot legally claim gluten-free) and skews lower-carb and sessionable by design (core ABVs 3.5–5.8%). Nobody is telling this story yet — it is live ammunition for the female market and moderating drinkers (D06 hypothesis H7).

## The vessels: keg formats

| Format | Detail | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Plastic bladder keg (proprietary)** | Pick up and go, no CO2 handling | The convenience default — ~70% of former corny customers have switched to it |
| **Corny keg (stainless)** | Drop by COB Tuesday → ready Friday; $3/keg cleaning fee | Serves existing corny-system owners — a switcher on-ramp |
| **Sizes** | 5L (mini/gift/birthday) · **20L (the standard)** · 50L (functions/wholesale) | 20L vs the industry's 50L = fresher beer, more variety per household |

## The taproom offer

- **Everyday:** 27 taps, tasters/schooners/jugs across Core/Premium/Top Shelf; tasting paddle 4×200ml ($18 sign / $20 screen — reconcile); "Tap That Knockoffs" happy hour Mon–Fri 3–5pm; Ammazza pizza $21 + packaged snacks; 22-foot shuffleboard; Hop On Brewery Tours groups.
- **Functions:** mezzanine ~40 / downstairs ~70 / full venue ~120–130 seated, standing to 250 (target); minimum-spend model benchmarked at 1 drink/person/hour — missed only once.
- **Events:** ticketed music nights (organiser takes door, venue takes bar) — one recent event did a week's trade in 5 hours; Brazilian night booked early September.

## Strengths and gaps

**Strengths:** award-backed brewing credibility in year one; the only range in category engineered for the veto-holder (1/3 non-beer); two keg formats spanning convenience and compatibility ("we fill any system"); hardware at cost with credit sweeteners; a membership that makes refill cadence measurable; Benchy as a zero-competitor wedge.

**Gaps:** **no packaged product (cans)** — the first of three blockers on the sports-club channel; **no kitchen or food licence** — caps functions and after-work trade; Benchy unpriced and unlaunched; the health/low-carb story unmarketed; keg system hire and venue hire are POA with no productised packages.
