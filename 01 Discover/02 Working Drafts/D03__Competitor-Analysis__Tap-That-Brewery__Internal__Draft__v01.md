# D03 — Competitor Analysis
## Tap That Brewery · Draft v01 · Internal · 19 Aug 2026

## Competitor groups (by type, not just name)

| Type | Who | What they sell | Where Tap That Brewery wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local keg-refill breweries** | Aardvark & Arrow* (literally across the road), Laughing Lizard (northern Gold Coast) | Similar keg/refill model, cheaper kegs ($85–90 vs $120–170) | Quality + consistency, award-backed brewing, 27-tap variety incl. non-beer, membership/loyalty ecosystem, the taproom as demo floor |
| **Online keg distributors** | Kegs Off Tap, Keg Republic | Commodity kegs shipped, wholesale positioning | Freshness (20L brewed on site), local relationship, service (Tue-drop/Fri-ready corny turnaround), community |
| **Bottleshops + big retail** | Dan Murphy's et al. | Packaged beer convenience | Price per litre (no excise, no wholesaler), freshness, the experience of tap at home |
| **Pubs/venues** | Local hospitality | The night out | Cost ("a week's trade in your garage"), no babysitter, host status |
| **Locked channel** | CUB-exclusive NRL venues | Tap contracts | Not winnable head-on — route around via non-affiliated clubs and white-label |

\* Spelling per handwritten workshop note ("across the road"); Circleback transcribed "Ardbach and Arrow." Confirm name and profile properly before Final.

## The two axes where Tap That Brewery can lead

1. **Freshness & range** (20L brewed-here variety + 27 taps incl. RTDs/seltzers) vs commodity 50L distribution.
2. **Ecosystem & belonging** (memberships, Tap Tokens, leaderboard, referral rewards, giveaways, tours, functions) vs transactional keg swaps.

Price is deliberately **not** an axis — Tap That Brewery is 40–90% dearer per keg than local rivals and must never fight on sticker price; it fights on per-litre economics vs retail/pub and on everything else vs keg rivals.

## Contrast lines (draft)

- Unlike the online keg warehouses, Tap That Brewery brews what you pour — 20L at a time, fresh, five minutes from where you drink it.
- Unlike the cheap keg swap across the road, Tap That Brewery is a range: 27 taps, a third of them for the people who don't drink beer.
- Unlike the pub, your garage doesn't have a closing time — or a babysitter surcharge.

## Category gap + proof

**Gap:** nobody is building the *installed-base refill brand* — every competitor sells kegs; none owns the relationship with the Gold Coast's existing keg system owners regardless of where the hardware came from.
**Proof it works:** ~50% of Tap That Brewery's refill customers switched from other suppliers with no targeted campaign; ~70% of corny-keg holdouts converted to Tap That Brewery's plastic system once they experienced it.

**Benchmark to beat:** Aardvark & Arrow — same suburb, same model, lower price. If the strategy can't articulate why a refill customer crosses the road *toward* Tap That Brewery, it isn't finished.

## One-sentence position (draft)

*Tap That Brewery is the Gold Coast's home-tap brand: award-winning beer and a 27-tap range, brewed in Burleigh, filling any keg system — because good times at your place beat a night out anywhere else.*
