The Biggest Surprise From The Entire Demographic Section

The biggest surprise wasn't age.

It wasn't postcode.

It wasn't Facebook.

It was how consistently the demographics matched the behavioural responses.

Everything points toward the same person.

And that's actually a sign of a healthy survey.

Often surveys produce conflicting customer profiles.

This one didn't.

The demographic data, consumption data, event data, referral data and open feedback all point toward essentially the same customer avatar.

That gives me quite a bit of confidence in the broader findings of the census.

My Overall Census Conclusion

If I had to summarise the entire survey in one sentence:

Tap That customers aren't buying beer on tap because they're craft beer enthusiasts; they're buying a more convenient, social and enjoyable way to drink at home.

And almost every major finding in the census supports that conclusion. 🍻
