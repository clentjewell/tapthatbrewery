The two axes where Tap That Brewery can lead

  1. Freshness & range (20L brewed-here variety + 27 taps incl. RTDs/seltzers) vs commodity 50L distribution.
  2. Ecosystem & belonging (memberships, Tap Tokens, leaderboard, referral rewards, giveaways, tours, functions) vs transactional keg swaps.

Price is deliberately not an axis – Tap That Brewery is 40–90% dearer per keg than local rivals and must never fight on sticker price; it fights on per-litre economics vs retail/pub and on everything else vs keg rivals.

Contrast lines (draft)

  • Unlike the online keg warehouses, Tap That Brewery brews what you pour – 20L at a time, fresh, five minutes from where you drink it.
  • Unlike the cheap keg swap across the road, Tap That Brewery is a range: 27 taps, a third of them for the people who don’t drink beer.
  • Unlike the pub, your garage doesn’t have a closing time – or a babysitter surcharge.

Category gap + proof

Gap: nobody is building the installed-base refill brand – every competitor sells kegs; none owns the relationship with the Gold Coast’s existing keg system owners regardless of where the hardware came from. Proof it works: ~50% of Tap That Brewery’s refill customers switched from other suppliers with no targeted campaign; ~70% of corny-keg holdouts converted to Tap That Brewery’s plastic system once they experienced it.

Benchmark to beat: Aardvark & Arrow – same suburb, same model, lower price. If the strategy can’t articulate why a refill customer crosses the road toward Tap That Brewery, it isn’t finished.

One-sentence position (draft)

Tap That Brewery is the Gold Coast’s home-tap brand: award-winning beer and a 27-tap range, brewed in Burleigh, filling any keg system – because good times at your place beat a night out anywhere else.
