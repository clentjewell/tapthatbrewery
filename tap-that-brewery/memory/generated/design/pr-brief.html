The job

Earned media that does what paid can't: credibility during the 3-month buying cycle and backlinks for #55. Three stories, pitched in this order. The Gold Coast Bulletin has already covered the founding ("Shaken but not stirred") — there is a proven local appetite; build on it, don't repeat it.

Story 1 — The comeback + the award (lead)

Angle: Head brewer Chris Smith was at Burleigh Barrels when it collapsed owing staff. He rebuilt across town — and Tap That Brewery's Midnight in Tokyo (black Japanese lager) took Brewer's Choice at Crafted in their first year entering. Redemption arc + hometown pride + a product you can taste this weekend. Hooks: award anniversary; next Crafted entry; new beer releases off the "Crafting Soon" board. Guardrails: Burleigh Barrels handled factually and generously — Chris's story, not a takedown; "Australia's biggest beer festival" is the client's description of Crafted — verify before any release uses it.

Story 2 — The Benchy novelty (colour/lifestyle)

Angle: "Beer on tap in the middle of nowhere" — a Gold Coast brewery is putting draught beer into 4WDs, campsites, boats and caravans with a portable tap no one else in the country offers. Strong visual story (hero imagery from #54); first partner (a local 4WD fit-out business) gives it a second local face. Hooks: partner launch; summer camping season; caravan/camping show calendar [dates TBC]. Guardrails: responsible-consumption framing is mandatory — never drink-driving-adjacent; lean on the social-responsibility flyer commitments (supports programs for victims of drink driving) proactively.

Story 3 — The club white-label (community/sport)

Angle: Local sports clubs get their own beer — "Surfers Paradise Demons Lager" model: white-labelled club beer, 10% player discount, 10% back to the club. A sponsorship story where the money stays in the community, against the backdrop of big-brewer tap exclusivity. Hooks: first signed club (#19 KR 3.2); season launch; grand-final season. Guardrails: pitch only once a club is signed and pouring — no vapourware; do not name CUB or attack exclusivity deals directly, let the contrast speak.
