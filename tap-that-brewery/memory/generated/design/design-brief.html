Design Brief

Pre-CP1 draft – produced ahead of Discover sign-off; census data, membership pricing reconciliation and competitor verification may revise inputs.

The job

Produce four collateral kits, in this order. Kit 4 (in-venue fix) ships immediately – it unblocks every other kit; nothing customer-facing publishes off unreconciled prices.

Brand constraints (all kits)

  • Existing logo and green/gold identity – no rebrand, no new marks.
  • Voice: cheeky, pun-forward, effort-free Aussie warmth ("Tap That Knockoffs", "Skittle Me This", "5 ways to support us – total $0.00"). No craft-beer pretension, no stock-photo gloss (photography per #54).
  • Working creative rule: taste and freshness lead, cost states itself plainly – the census ranks taste 2.19 and cost 2.75, so price maths belongs on open-feed pieces, not held for the close. Connection (3.61) is a retention argument.
  • All prices from the reconciled price list (Kit 4 output). Claims discipline: gluten-reduced never gluten-free; "Australia’s biggest beer festival" unverified – use "Brewer’s Choice at Crafted" until confirmed.

Kit 2 – Giveaway kit (next cycle)

The proven engine: ~1,000 entries/cycle, 50%-off close converts 22–25 systems – target 35+ with digital nurture (#19 KR 1.2).

  • Entry promo set (Meta + IG story + in-venue QR poster), entry-form header, 4 × nurture email headers (feeds #57), 50%-off close creative, winner-announcement template.
  • Entry mechanic must capture email + mobile + "own a system already? Y/N" (feeds switcher lane).

  • Product one-pager (A5, for 4WD fit-out counter – first partner: Harry’s cousin’s business), social launch set, partner co-brand template, simple spec/price card [Benchy pricing TBC – currently unpriced].

Kit 4 – In-venue pricing & membership refresh (IMMEDIATE)

The two live membership posters conflict ($300 + $120/yr renewal vs $250/yr; token values differ). The keg systems card independently supports the newer poster – but the client must confirm which is live before print.

  • One reconciled membership poster (Keg Crew / Mug Club / Brew Buds), dated; old poster physically removed.
  • Tasting paddle price fixed across sign and screen ($18 vs $20 – client to call it).
  • Add award signage (Midnight in Tokyo – Brewer’s Choice at Crafted) at the tap and on the takeaway price board – currently absent, per #12.
  • Refresh keg systems + accessories card only if prices change.

Success criteria

  1. Kit 4 installed and photographed with zero price conflicts remaining in venue.
  2. Kits 1–3 delivered as layered source files + export packs sized for Meta, print and web; every asset passes the voice check (would Harry post it?) and the claims discipline above.
  3. No asset leads with sticker-price comparison against local keg rivals (Tap That Brewery is 40–90% dearer and never fights there).

Deadlines
