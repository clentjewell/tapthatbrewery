Social Strategy

Pre-CP1 draft – produced ahead of Discover sign-off; census data, membership pricing reconciliation and competitor verification may revise inputs.

The job social does here

Social is not a brand-awareness hobby – it is the top of the refill funnel and the nurture layer for a 3-month buying cycle (43% take 3+ months; 34% buy inside a month – 10% immediately plus 24% within it). Every post either recruits a future system owner, nurtures one who’s considering, or reminds an existing owner to refill. The whole strategy is sized for one operator: Raef, at ~70% marketing allocation, who also covers bar shifts. If a tactic needs more than ~6–8 hours a week, it is not in this plan.

Content pillars (mapped to brand pillars)

Five pillars, matching the brand architecture. Working ratio across a month: roughly 2 : 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Taste and freshness lead the argument (census driver #1 at 2.19); cost runs in the open rather than waiting for retargeting (2.75, second); connection carries retention rather than the sale (3.61).

Content pillar Brand pillar What it looks like
1. Good times at yours Connection Backyards, decks, sheds – hosting moments at customers' places. The night out that came home. No babysitter, no closing time
2. The brewery floor Taste/Freshness Chris brewing, the "Kegged & Ready / Crafting Soon" board, 20L freshness vs commodity 50L, the Midnight in Tokyo award story
3. Meet the range Taste + partner buy-in Tap profiles across 27 taps – deliberately ~1 in 3 posts from the non-beer third (seltzers, gin fizz sours, ginger beer, cocktails). This pillar answers "my wife won’t let me" in public
4. Kudos builds & UGC Kudos + Connection Install showcases, shed/outdoor-build reveals, Benchy rigs – real customers on camera, not the owners. The esky-to-envy arc
5. Deals that close Cost Giveaways, the 50%-off close, referral rewards, happy hour "Knockoffs", Keg Crew value. Capped at ~1 post in 5 so cost never becomes the brand

Voice rules

  1. Pun-forward, never smug. The house voice is already on the walls: "Tap That Knockoffs", "Skittle Me This", "Slice Spice Baby". Write like the venue signs, not like an ad agency.
  2. Cost never opens. Kudos and connection do the buying; per-litre maths appears in comments, captions' second half, and retargeting – not headlines.
  3. Aussie warmth, zero gatekeeping. The "5 ways to support us – total $0.00" energy: generous, self-aware, community-first.
  4. Moderation is on-brand, not a disclaimer. "Fresher, better – not more." The census line (57% of owners who pour daily or most days pour only 1–2 kegs a month) is a brag, not a caveat.
  5. Specifics over adjectives. "44 schooners in a 20L keg" beats "great value". Real tap names, real prices, real customers.

The UGC engine (real customers, not owners)

Discovery named it directly: UGC from real customers is identified but under-executed, and the owners should get off camera for this pillar. The engine:

Step Mechanic
Source Every install/delivery ends with the ask: "send us a video of the first pour at yours." Giveaway entry terms include a content-permission tick-box. Branded prompt: #TapThatAtMine
Rights Lightweight and written: a saved DM template requesting repost permission + credit; giveaway T&Cs grant usage for entrants' submitted content. No repost without a yes
Reward Pay in the existing currency – Tap Tokens (proposed: 250 tokens per approved repost; 500 for a shot we use in ads – values to be ratified against the 1,000/1,000/500 referral schedule). Feeds the public leaderboard (top holder 7,569 points), which is itself content
Amplify Best UGC each month becomes the pillar-4 hero post + an ad creative candidate + a website testimonial (#42 repurposing flow)

Giveaway amplification (2×/yr, the proven engine)

The giveaway is the brand’s biggest organic moment – though only the fifth acquisition channel at 10%, behind taproom 22%, Google 20%, referral 18% and social 16% (~1,000 entries/cycle, 50%-off close converting 22–25 systems). Social’s job per cycle: launch post + weekly countdown stories + share-to-enter mechanics on Facebook + entrant UGC prompts + a filmed winner install (which seeds pillar 4 for the next quarter) + the close-offer push to non-winners. What to avoid – AU alcohol marketing rules (ABAC + platform)

  • Age-gate both profiles 18+ and apply alcohol audience restrictions on any boosted post.
  • No excess-consumption content ever: no skolling, no drinking games, no intoxication played for laughs, no "session all day" framing. The brand’s own census makes moderation the story.
  • Nobody who appears under 25 shown drinking or holding product in brand or reposted UGC – screen UGC for this before reposting.
  • No alcohol + operation of vehicles: Benchy/4WD content shows camp set up, vehicle parked, day done. Never a pour in or beside a moving rig or boat underway.
  • No therapeutic or success claims: beer doesn’t relax you, fix your week, or make you popular. "Gluten-reduced" only – never "gluten-free" (legally unavailable); carb claims need compliance wording locked (#42, Gap 3) before publishing.
  • Responsible-service framing on promotions: giveaway and discount T&Cs carry standard responsible-consumption lines; no offers that reward volume drinking speed or quantity consumed.
