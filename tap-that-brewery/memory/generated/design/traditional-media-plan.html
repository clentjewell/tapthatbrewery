Traditional Media Plan

Pre-CP1 draft — produced ahead of Discover sign-off; census data, membership pricing reconciliation and competitor verification may revise inputs.

The honest assessment

Traditional media has a limited role at current budget — and that is the finding, not a hedge. The business is loss-making, has never proven a single paid digital channel, cannot yet attribute a walk-in to a message (#48 provenance capture is not built), and its two proven acquisition engines — giveaways and the taproom — are measurable in ways radio and print are not. Buying broadcast reach before the measurable channels are proven would repeat the taproom mistake in media form: spending on what is visible instead of what converts.

Recommendation: hold all paid traditional media until paid digital (#46) has survived its own kill criteria for two full giveaway cycles. The exceptions below are the narrow cases where traditional formats do a job digital cannot, ranked by cost and fit. Dollar figures are deliberately [TBC] throughout — no traditional buy should be priced before the digital baseline exists to compare it against.

What is explicitly ruled out at current budget

Television, outdoor billboards, cinema, general-awareness print schedules, and any always-on radio presence. Reasons: unattributable at this scale, priced for advertisers with margin to burn, and aimed at a 12-month brand-building horizon the P&L cannot currently fund. Revisit only after profitability (#18 measure 2) or a funded Stage 3 (#23).

Measurement and kill criteria

Attribution mechanics are mandatory, not optional: unique offer codes per medium, "how did you hear about us" captured at the counter into the #48 provenance field, and coded URLs/QRs on anything printed. Kill criteria: any traditional test that cannot demonstrate entries, enquiries or first fills at a cost within striking distance of the digital equivalent is not repeated — one test each, no "give it time to build" second buys. Signage and livery are exempt (infrastructure, one-off), but even they get a provenance tally.
