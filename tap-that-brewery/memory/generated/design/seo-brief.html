The job

Own search for the category nobody owns: "beer on tap at home" on the Gold Coast, plus the refill intent of the installed base. The 3-month buying cycle means most buyers research repeatedly before purchase – search is where the nurture happens when the venue isn’t in front of them. The first 6 content pieces (in order)

  1. Keg cost calculator – interactive (per #53): kegs/month → $ vs bottle-o vs pub (~$2.70/schooner maths). The link-earning asset; cost content is fine in search because the searcher raised it (dirty-little-secret rule governs ads, not answers).
  2. "Which keg system is right for you?" – 2-tap ($975) to 6-tap ($2,550) chooser, at-cost explanation, Afterpay/Zip, space + partner objections answered inline.
  3. The switcher guide – "Already own a keg system? Here’s how refills work with us": Tue-drop→Fri-ready, plastic vs corny, we-fill-any-system promise.
  4. Keg care basics – line cleaning, keeping beer fresh, why 20L stays fresher than 50L. Service content for owners who bought elsewhere.
  5. Party planning maths – how many kegs for X guests, keg system hire, the 1-drink-per-person-per-hour benchmark from functions experience.
  6. Benchy / beer off-grid – portable taps for 4WD, camping, boating; partner content hooks for fit-out businesses.

Each piece: one target cluster, FAQ schema, internal links to its money page, a capture CTA (calculator → giveaway/demo).

GBP & local actions

  • Claim/clean the Google Business Profile: categories (Brewery + Beer store), services list including "keg refills" and "keg system sales", full price attributes, weekly photo cadence (Harry, from #54 library), events posted (#60), Q&A seeded with the four objections.
  • Review engine: post-refill review ask (via #57/#58 flows); respond to all reviews in brand voice.
  • Local citations: consistent NAP across GC directories; Hop On Brewery Tours + partner backlinks (Smoked Barbecue Co, Never Quit Gym, Ammazza).

Technical checklist

  • One reconciled price source feeding all pages (no conflicting prices indexed – the poster problem must not become a SERP problem).
  • LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ, Event schema; clean URL structure per #53; XML sitemap; sub-2.5s LCP mobile.
  • Age-gate implemented without blocking crawl (interstitial, not wall).
  • GA4 events wired to organic landing pages so cluster → demo/giveaway conversion is reportable.
