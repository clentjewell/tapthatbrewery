Page Strategy

Evidence note (19 Aug 2026). Figures in this draft predate the client's census and planning documents. Several have been corrected; some interpretations are still under revision. Read 20A Evidence Reconciliation before citing anything here.

Pre-CP1 draft — produced ahead of Discover sign-off; census data, membership pricing reconciliation and competitor verification may revise inputs.

House rules across all six pages: kudos/connection open, cost closes (Christy's rule); every price sits next to an Afterpay/Zip mark; every capture lands in Fishbowl (or an exportable list until integration ships); voice is the venue's own — cheeky, pun-forward, zero corporate. Copy drafts are downstream; this fixes what each page must argue and in what order.

1. Home
Purpose Route four visitor types in one scroll; open the "beer on tap at home" category claim
Segments All — triage page
Message hierarchy 1. Good times at your place (connection/kudos) → 2. Award-winning beer brewed in Burleigh, 27 taps, a third not even beer → 3. System at cost, refills that beat the bottle-o per litre (cost, quietly)
Proof Crafted Brewer's Choice (first year), tap-wall imagery, Tap Token leaderboard/community signals, real-customer UGC when available
CTAs Primary: enter the giveaway / join Brew Buds. Secondary: explore keg systems
Objections handled None head-on — Home routes; objection pages answer
Blocks in order Hero (backyard pour, category line) → 4-door router (own a system / get refills–we fill any system / visit the taproom / book a function) → award + brewed-in-Burleigh strip → range teaser incl. non-beer third → giveaway capture → membership/community teaser → footer

2. Keg Systems
Purpose Host the 3-month consideration; make the at-cost model make sense; get the demo booked
Segments Young families (~45%), tradies (~20%), boomers (~20%); partner as reader-over-the-shoulder
Message hierarchy 1. Your place becomes the place (connection/kudos) → 2. We sell the system at cost — the same price as buying direct — because we're a brewery, not a hardware shop → 3. From $975, Afterpay/Zip, lease-to-buy coming
Proof At-cost pricing shown transparently ($975/$1,250/$1,950/$2,550, bonus credits $40–100); census stat (57% pour daily or most days, yet average just ~1.55 kegs a month (census)); install photos/UGC; 20–30% of taproom visitors buy one after seeing it
CTAs Primary: book a demo. Secondary: enter the giveaway (for the not-yet-ready majority)
Objections Upfront cost (at-cost + payment options + per-litre maths); space (size/footprint guide per system); "I'll drink too much" (census stat, 20L freshness logic)
Blocks in order Hero (the pour, the mates) → why at cost (the honest model) → system range cards with prices → "which one's you" guide (2-tap starter → 6-tap shed build) → objections block → demo booking → giveaway fallback capture

3. We Fill Any System
Purpose Convert the installed base — owners who bought hardware elsewhere — straight to refills; the cheapest customer in the business
Segments Behavioural: any GC keg system owner. 116 of 206 owners (56%) arrived this way unprompted
Message hierarchy 1. Whoever sold you the system, we'll keep it flowing → 2. Award-winning beer, 27 taps of choice, brewed five minutes from where you drink it → 3. Member pricing makes it routine ($120 core 20L vs $150)
Proof Award; brewed-on-site freshness (20L); corny service promise (drop COB Tuesday, ready Friday, $3 clean); half our refill family switched to us
CTAs Primary: order/book your first refill. Secondary: join Keg Crew (subject to price reconciliation)
Objections "Is my system compatible?" (plain answer + corny/plastic explainer); "I already have a supplier" (freshness/range/club — never sticker price; Tap That Brewery is dearer than the $85–90 rivals and doesn't apologise for it)
Blocks in order Hero ("Got a keg system? We fill that.") → compatibility/how-it-works (plastic vs corny) → tap range strip incl. non-beer → refill pricing, member vs non-member → service promises → first-refill CTA → membership close

4. Refills & Beer Range
Purpose Shopfront for the ~$200/month engine; reorder home for 206 owners / 96–113 active; partner-approval exhibit
Segments Current customers (reorder), high-volume drinkers (the maths), the partner/veto-holder (the non-beer third)
Message hierarchy 1. Fresh, brewed here, always something new (taste/freshness) → 2. Something for everyone on the wall — seltzers, gin sours, mojitos, ginger beer → 3. Member maths (convenience/cost)
Proof The tap list itself (Midnight in Tokyo 4.6%, Czech Pilsner, Hazy Pale, sours, seltzers, RTDs, cocktails); "Kegged & Ready / Crafting Soon" pipeline as freshness proof; gluten-reduced, lower-carb by design
CTAs Primary: order/reserve a refill (GoTab). Secondary: join Keg Crew for $30 off every refill
Objections Partner veto ("here's what's on tap for her"); staleness/boredom (rotating limiteds); health hesitation (gluten-reduced/lower-carb — first marketed use of this held ammunition)
Blocks in order Tap list (filterable: beers / not-beers / limited) → coming soon board → refill pricing table (core/premium/top-shelf × member/non-member) → plastic vs corny + delivery when live → member maths → order CTA

5. Memberships & Tap Tokens
Purpose Lock refill cadence and make loyalty legible; the retention page
Segments New system owners (join at purchase), switchers, boomers (strongest membership fit), taproom regulars (Mug Club)
Message hierarchy 1. You're not a customer, you're crew (connection/kudos) → 2. The maths: $30 off every refill repays $250 in about four months at two kegs a month → 3. Tokens, leaderboard, birthday keg, referrals
Proof Live leaderboard (top holder 7,569 points at 1/8/26); perk list photo-verified (birthday 5L, mugs at schooner prices, tasting nights, Never Quit Gym cross-perk); referral rewards (1,000 tokens per system referral, 500 per refill referral)
CTAs Primary: join Keg Crew. Secondary: join Brew Buds free (mailing-list capture — never leave the page empty-handed)
Objections "Another subscription?" (payback maths, no lock-in framing to confirm); "which tier?" (three-tier comparison, plainly)
Blocks in order Crew ethos hero → three-tier comparison table → the Keg Crew payback maths → Tap Tokens + leaderboard + referral rewards → join CTA → Brew Buds fallback
Blocker Do not build until pricing reconciled ($250/yr poster vs $300 + $120/yr renewal poster)

6. Functions & Venue Hire
Purpose Fill the venue's dead capacity with minimum-spend bookings and keg-system hire — without taproom gravity retaking the site
Segments Workplaces (Burleigh industrial-estate neighbours), birthdays/private parties, PR agencies (launch activations — untapped), wedding-adjacent
Message hierarchy 1. A working brewery as your venue — mezzanine to the whole floor (kudos) → 2. Minimum spend, not hire fees — one drink per person per hour and it takes care of itself → 3. Can't come to us? Keg system hire brings the pub to you
Proof Capacity (mezzanine ~40, downstairs ~70, ~120–130 seated, 109 standing recently); only missed minimum spend once; event track record (a week's trade in 5 hours); 22-ft shuffleboard, working brewhouse backdrop
CTAs Primary: function enquiry form (date, headcount, occasion — structured so leads don't leak like wholesale DMs did). Secondary: keg system hire enquiry
Objections Food (honest answer: Ammazza pizza + snacks, food-truck options — set expectations, don't hide the no-kitchen reality); "is it just a bar?" (whole-venue photos, event formats)
Blocks in order Hero (full mezzanine mid-event) → spaces + capacities → how minimum spend works → food story → keg system hire ("bring the pub to you") → enquiry form

Assumptions & open items

  • All "current site" comparisons [pending site audit]; blocks above are target-state.
  • Membership page gated on price reconciliation; lease-to-buy and delivery copy gated on GoTab go-lives.
  • UGC proof elements depend on the customer-content push (identified in discovery, underexecuted) — placeholder imagery until then.
  • Tap list freshness mechanism (manual vs GoTab-fed) to be resolved; a stale "live" tap list is worse than none.
