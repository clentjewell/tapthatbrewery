Events Activation

Pre-CP1/CP2 draft — Deploy runbooks are written ahead of both gates. Nothing here should be executed until the Design plan it descends from is approved.

What the evidence changed

Two findings from the client's material reset this runbook:

  1. Format is not the variable — the organiser is. Music nights, birthdays, engagements, corporate networking and seasonal tastings work. Trivia, musical bingo, open mic and stand-up comedy have all failed here. The pattern is that events succeed when someone else brings the room. Book organisers, not concepts.
  2. 73% of members do not attend seasonal releases. The tasting-night perk is real and largely unredeemed. Saturday afternoon is the preferred slot for 73% of those who would attend — the current scheduling is part of the problem.

A third finding governs demos: 63% of respondents would host a "bring a mate" demo at their own place. That is the single strongest event signal in the census and it is not a venue event at all.

Measurement hooks

Per event: attendance, entries captured, contacts consented, systems sold within 90 days, bar revenue as a secondary line. Monthly into #26: taproom-sourced actives (the taproom is 22% of acquisition — the largest single channel) and demo-sourced conversions.

Stop conditions

An event format failing twice does not get a third run · any event without ID and RSA discipline stops immediately · Bring a Mate pauses if a host offer is being claimed without a demo actually occurring.
