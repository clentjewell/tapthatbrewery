Why this exists before any results do

Case studies die from evidence not captured at the time. Two of this engagement's stories will be worth telling — but only if the baselines are frozen now, before Deploy moves them, and the capture mechanics run from the first campaign day. This pack names the stories, the numbers, the mechanics and the skeleton. Every results field below is deliberately empty and stays empty until Deploy actuals land.

Write-up skeleton (fill only with Deploy actuals)

  1. Context — the at-cost hardware model, margin in refills, 206 owners / 96–113 active vs 1,000 target, loss-making P&L, one 70% marketer.
  2. Problem — [Story A: acquisition cost / Story B: the nurture gap]. Stated in the client's own words from discovery.
  3. What we did — the intervention, its cost, its build time, who did what (R/J/C split).
  4. Results — [EMPTY: actives added, conversion vs baseline, cost per active, save/winback rates, revenue run-rate delta]. Every number sourced to a filed export.
  5. Proof texture — quotes (released), before/after screenshots, the repeatability point (cycle 2 vs cycle 1).
  6. What it means — the replicable playbook, honestly caveated (what was luck, what was system).
