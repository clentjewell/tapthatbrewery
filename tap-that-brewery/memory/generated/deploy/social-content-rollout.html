Social Content Rollout

Pre-CP1/CP2 draft – Deploy runbooks are written ahead of both gates. Nothing here should be executed until the Design plan it descends from is approved.

The operating rhythm

Three posts and three to four stories a week across Instagram and Facebook, from one monthly batch shoot. Facebook is not the secondary platform here – 54% of census respondents name it as their primary channel against Instagram’s 38%, and the base skews 41–60 (65% of respondents). Facebook gets the same effort, not the leftovers, and copy is written for people who read captions rather than scan Reels.

Everything below runs at ~6–8 hours a week. If it takes more, the plan is wrong, not the operator.

The referral-awareness fix – first content priority

Tap Tokens awareness is 91.7%. Awareness of what the tokens are for collapses: keg-system referral 43.8%, refill referral 29.2%, venue referral 4.2%, trade 2.1%. This is the cheapest available growth lever and it is a communication failure, not a program failure.

Step Action Owner Timing
1 One pinned post per platform stating the three rewards plainly (1,000 system · 1,000 venue · 500 refill) Harry/Harry Week 1 of rollout
2 Monthly "deals that close" slot – leaderboard shout-out, one member story Harry/Harry Ongoing
3 Story series: "what your tokens are actually worth" Harry/Harry Month 2
4 Re-measure awareness at the next census Jewell Next survey

Target: system-referral awareness from 43.8% to ≥70% within two quarters.

Publishing standards

  • Every post: 18+ audience setting on both platforms, responsible-consumption tone, no consumption-rate or health claims, no under-25-looking talent, no vehicle-adjacent drinking imagery (Benchy content shows the vehicle parked, per ABAC).
  • Price posts: non-member price first, member price second and labelled. The $2.98 / $2.34 rule from #66 applies to organic identically.
  • UGC: written permission captured before reposting – a story reply screenshot is enough, filed in the UGC folder.
  • Comments: answered within one business day; price and "will it fit" questions get the calculator link, not a debate.

Event and campaign overlays

Campaign windows (#66) add content on top of this cadence, they don’t replace it. During a giveaway window Harry/Harry runs launch film, countdowns and the winner install – his proven lane – while the five pillars keep turning underneath at reduced volume (drop to 2 posts/week if needed, never to zero).

Measurement hooks

Reported monthly, four lines only: reach and follower change by platform, saves and shares on build showcases (the strongest system-intent signal available organically), link clicks to the calculator and range pages, and referral-mechanic mentions or claims. Vanity engagement rate is not reported.

Stop conditions

  • Any post drawing responsible-service complaints comes down within the hour and goes to Jewell for review before anything else publishes.
  • If the queue empties twice in a quarter, the cadence is formally reduced in #45 rather than quietly missed.
