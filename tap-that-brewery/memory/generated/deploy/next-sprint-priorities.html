The shape of the next 90 days

Three sprints of four weeks, three items each. That cap is not conservatism – it is the arithmetic of one marketer at ~70% capacity who also works the bar, plus Jewell build support and founder time measured in hours, not days.

Everything here is ordered by a single principle: fix what is wrong, instrument what is unmeasured, then spend. The client’s existing $1,500/month Meta budget does not start moving on a new plan until sprint 2, because spending against unverified tracking is how the last agency’s data became unreadable.

Gate dependencies

Sprints 1 and 2 need no gate: tracking, account recovery, error correction and awareness work are all valid regardless of which strategy is approved. Sprint 3 needs CP2, because paid creative and lifecycle content express the messaging architecture the census may still revise.

That split is intentional. It means the first eight weeks of Deploy can start the day the client says go, while the strategy questions – which plan is live, is cost on or off the table, Model A or Model B – get the founders' session they need.
