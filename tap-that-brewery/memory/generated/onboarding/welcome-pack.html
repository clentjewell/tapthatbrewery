How the engagement runs

We work in three phases – Discover, Design, Deploy – each closed by a checkpoint you sign before the next begins.

Stage What happens Where it ends
Discover (underway) We lock the validated facts: your audience, competitors, offers, pricing and priority problems. The 19 August session and site visit did most of this CP1 – Discovery readout session; you sign the Discover Summary
Design The full strategy: brand, messaging, customer journey, channel plans and production-ready briefs – built only on what you signed at CP1 CP2 – Strategy presentation; you sign the Strategy Summary and Approved Roadmap
Deploy Execution of the approved roadmap: builds, launches, automation, content, reporting. Scoped and agreed at CP2 Ongoing delivery and review rhythm

What happens next, in order:

  1. Now: you send the checklist items below; we finish the Discover documents.
  2. CP1 (Discovery readout): ~90-minute session with both founders. We present the full picture of the business as we’ve validated it; you correct anything wrong and sign.
  3. Design phase: weekly check-ins with Raef; founders pulled in for a small number of working inputs.
  4. CP2 (Strategy presentation): the strategy, the roadmap, and the Deploy scope and pricing. You sign, and we start building.

What "sign-off" means

Two documents in this engagement carry a signature: the Discover Summary (CP1) and the Strategy Summary + Approved Roadmap (CP2) – plus the Proposal that starts it all.

  • Signing means: this is accurate, this is agreed, build on it. It protects you – nothing gets designed on facts you haven’t confirmed, and nothing gets built you haven’t approved.
  • One named person on your side signs (agree who at kick-off – our default suggestion is Justin, with Chris consulted on anything brand or product).
  • After sign-off, a document is locked. New information doesn’t reopen it informally – it becomes a versioned revision, so there’s always one agreed source of truth.
  • Sign-offs are recorded in the engagement folder under Approvals.
