Client Context — Tap That Brewery

The business

Brewery, taproom and home keg system business in Burleigh Heads, Gold Coast, Queensland. Sells keg systems at cost and earns its margin on 20L keg refills. Runs a 27-tap taproom, roughly a third of it non-beer, and a small wholesale channel.

Brand voice

Cheeky and pun-forward, with straight talk underneath. Observed in venue: “Tap That Knockoffs” for happy hour, “Skittle Me This” and “Bone Water IPA” on the sours, and a receipt-styled sign listing five ways to support the business totalling $0.00. Warm, Australian, unpretentious. Never craft-beer-snob: their own core customer is described as “not a craft beer fanatic”.

Their own words for the business: “We’re in the business of connection and good times — keg systems and refills are the means, not the end.”
