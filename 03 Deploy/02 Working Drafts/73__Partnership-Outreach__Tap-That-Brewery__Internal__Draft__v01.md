## The rule, restated as an operating constraint

**No more than two pilots run at once.** Follow-up capacity is one marketer at ~70% plus founder time. The failure mode this business already has is leads dying between first reply and manager contact – adding partners faster than they can be answered reproduces it at scale.

Each pilot needs three things before first contact: a named owner, a one-page offer, and a pilot metric with a date.

## Outreach sequence (per partner)

| Step | Action | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research: what they sell, who their customer is, what the offer does for **their** margin | Day 0 | Jewell |
| 2 | One-page offer written from the partner’s side of the table | Day 1 | Jewell |
| 3 | First contact – founder to owner where possible; DM is acceptable to open, never to negotiate | Day 3 | Justin or Harry/Harry |
| 4 | **Reply logged the same day in the partner tracker.** This is the step that currently fails | Day 3+ | Harry/Harry |
| 5 | Meeting at the venue – the tap wall does half the selling | Day 7–14 | Justin |
| 6 | Written pilot terms: what each side supplies, the metric, the review date | Day 14 | Jewell |
| 7 | Launch, with a diarised 30/60/90 check | Day 21 | Owner |
| 8 | Review against the pilot metric; formalise, adjust or end | Day 90 | Jewell + Justin |

Nothing skips step 6. A partnership without written terms is a favour, and favours end when the person who arranged them is busy.

## Referral program activation – the highest-return item here

The mechanics exist and are on a wall sign. The problem is entirely awareness:

| Reward | Awareness | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Tap Tokens generally | 91.7% | Maintain |
| **Keg system referral (1,000)** | **43.8%** | ≥70% |
| **Refill referral (500)** | **29.2%** | ≥60% |
| **Venue referral (1,000)** | **4.2%** | ≥30% |
| Trade referral | 2.1% | ≥20% |

Referral already delivers **18% of acquisition** while fewer than half the base knows the main reward exists. Doubling awareness is cheaper than any campaign in this pack.

## Measurement hooks

Per pilot: contacts made, replies, meetings, pilot live Y/N, and the pilot metric at 90 days. Programme-level into #26: partner-sourced actives, referral redemptions by type, and referral awareness at the next census.

## Stop conditions

Three pilots live at once – pause the newest · a reply older than 48 hours unanswered – outreach stops until the tracker is clear · any white-label conversation with a CUB-exclusive venue · any partner offer quoting membership pricing before reconciliation.
