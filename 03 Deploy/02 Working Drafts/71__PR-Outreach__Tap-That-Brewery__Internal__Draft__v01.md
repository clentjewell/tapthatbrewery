## Posture

Earned only. No retainer, no wire, no spend beyond hours. One pitch cycle per quarter, four to six outlets per cycle – a solo marketer plus founder interview time cannot sustain more, and a thin list worked properly beats a broad list blasted.

## Interview discipline

Founders speak; Jewell never speaks for the business. Before any interview: the fact sheet is re-read, the unresolved active-customer number is off the table, and no forward revenue or customer-count target is given as a commitment. "We’re building toward" is a sentence; "we will have 1,000 by" is not, until #19 has a founder-confirmed date.

## Crisis communication framework

This was absent from #49 and is required. Alcohol businesses carry three predictable exposures.

| Scenario | First hour | Spokesperson | Holding line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Advertising complaint** (ABAC or price claim) | Pull the asset across every channel; screenshot what ran; do not delete the post without capturing it | Justin | "We’ve paused the material while we review it. We’ll correct anything that’s wrong." |
| **Product or safety issue** (contamination, gas, equipment) | Stop sale of the affected batch or unit; contact affected customers directly before any public statement | Chris, on production facts | "We’ve contacted every customer who may be affected and we’re dealing with it directly." |
| **Responsible-service incident** | Cooperate fully with the regulator; no public comment on an open matter | Justin | "We’re working with the authorities and won’t comment while that’s under way." |
| **Staff or founder-history story** | Do not relitigate the Burleigh Barrels history in public; the published account stands | Justin | Refer to the published piece; add nothing |

Standing rules: one spokesperson per incident, decided in the first ten minutes. Never "no comment" – always a holding line with a next step. Nothing goes out that hasn’t been read aloud by a second person. Social comments get the holding line, not a debate, and paid media pauses for the duration.

## Measurement hooks

Per cycle: pitches sent, responses, placements, and estimated audience. Annually: whether coverage correlates with taproom traffic (22% of acquisition arrives via the taproom, so a lift is detectable at the counter). PR is not attributed to sales – it is judged on placements and on whether the story assets earned re-use in EDM and social.

## Stop conditions

Any unverified figure in a pitch · any franchise story before founder consent · any moderation claim phrased as a health benefit.

## Dependencies

Founder consent on the franchise story (open item #6) · census raw-data verification for any public statistic · crisis contact list · image bank cleared for editorial use.
