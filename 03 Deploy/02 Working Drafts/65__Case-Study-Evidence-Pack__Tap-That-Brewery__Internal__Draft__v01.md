## Why this exists before any results do

Case studies die from evidence not captured at the time. Two of this engagement's stories will be worth telling — but only if the baselines are frozen **now**, before Deploy moves them, and the capture mechanics run from the first campaign day. This pack names the stories, the numbers, the mechanics and the skeleton. Every results field below is deliberately empty and stays empty until Deploy actuals land.

## The two candidate stories

| | Story A — The switcher campaign | Story B — The giveaway-nurture cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Working title | "We fill any system: acquiring refill customers without selling hardware" | "From 22 to [n]: what a 90-day nurture does to a proven giveaway" |
| The setup | 50% of refill customers bought hardware elsewhere and switched unprompted; nobody owned the installed base | 2×/yr giveaways pull ~1,000 entries; the 50%-off close converts 22–25 systems; the other ~975 entrants got silence |
| The intervention | A named lane: landing page, Meta behavioural targeting, first-fill offer, counter conversion (C1, Sep–Oct 2026) | A designed 90-day sequence matched to the 3-month buying cycle: objection ladder → close window (C2 Oct–Dec 2026, C3 Jan–Feb 2027) |
| Hypothesis proven (if it works) | H1 (switchers beat new-owner CAC) + H2 (kudos opens, cost closes) | H3 (structured nurture lifts conversion past 22–25; target 35+) |
| The headline number, when real | Cost per new active vs the hardware-giveaway route; +[n] actives with $0 hardware margin sacrificed | Systems per cycle vs the 22–25 baseline, across two cycles (repeatability is the story) |

## Capture mechanics (run from day one, owned by the monthly report rhythm)

| Mechanic | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| GoTab exports | Weekly actives/at-risk/churned counts; per-campaign transaction pulls; the dated day-one baseline export | Weekly, filed to `03 Deploy/01 Inputs/evidence/` |
| Screenshots | Old conflicting posters (before), reconciled collateral (after); manual SMS thread; leaderboard; ads manager results panels; nurture flow builds; entry-count dashboards | At each before/after moment — the "before" shot is the one people forget |
| Fishbowl/ESP reports | Sequence sends, opens, clicks, and the truth metric (transactions in window) per #47 | Per cycle |
| Customer quotes **with signed releases** | Switchers ("why'd you cross the road?"), giveaway buyers, winback returns; capture rides the #54 shoot releases and the content-permission tick on entry forms — no quote is usable without one | Ongoing; log quote + release together |
| Photo/video | Winner installs, backyard sessions, Benchy rigs — the #54 library doubles as case-study art | Per shoot |
| Cost records | Paid spend per campaign, prize/discount cost per cycle, Jewell + Raef hours — CAC claims need the denominator | Monthly |

## Write-up skeleton (fill only with Deploy actuals)

1. **Context** — the at-cost hardware model, margin in refills, 220 actives vs 1,000 target, loss-making P&L, one 70% marketer.
2. **Problem** — [Story A: acquisition cost / Story B: the nurture gap]. Stated in the client's own words from discovery.
3. **What we did** — the intervention, its cost, its build time, who did what (R/J/C split).
4. **Results** — [EMPTY: actives added, conversion vs baseline, cost per active, save/winback rates, revenue run-rate delta]. Every number sourced to a filed export.
5. **Proof texture** — quotes (released), before/after screenshots, the repeatability point (cycle 2 vs cycle 1).
6. **What it means** — the replicable playbook, honestly caveated (what was luck, what was system).
