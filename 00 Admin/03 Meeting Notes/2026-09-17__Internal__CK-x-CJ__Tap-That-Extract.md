## Usable: the repositioning argument

Christy's central point, and the sharpest strategic material in the meeting.

- **"Every household deserves beer on tap" is a business ambition, not a brand
  idea.** It states the goal. It does not carry the emotional lift the product
  actually delivers.
- **Tap That owns the top of the bottom.** It takes an ordinary social occasion
  and makes it spectacular. That is the promise.
- **The category is home entertainment, not beer.** The comparison set is coffee
  machines and outdoor kitchens: aspirational upgrades people choose to display.
  Christy's analogy is BMW moving from "cars" to "mobility" — it widens the
  product and partnership universe.
- **Social connection sits high on Maslow. The hardware looks like it sits at the
  bottom.** The design and the communications have to reflect the higher driver.
- **The proof of the gap:** kegerators do not appear in Harvey Norman's outdoor
  living section beside the sleek outdoor kitchens. They sit in appliances. The
  category is not yet read as aspirational.
- **The benchmark:** a Zip HydroTap sells for around $10,000 largely because it
  looks beautiful on a bench. The current keg units are purely functional and do
  not belong in that world.

## Usable: distribution and referral

- **Trades as a sales force.** Plumbers and kitchen installers are in the room
  when the decision is made. A card or QR code, and free beer or a kickback for
  every referral that converts.
- **Outdoor kitchen manufacturers.** Get a small keg-in-fridge tap unit specified
  as a standard inclusion alongside the fridge. Develop the visuals first and
  test demand on social before anything is manufactured.
- **Value H2O.** Around **10,000** distribution points up and down the east
  coast, **10** service technicians already in the field, predominantly small and
  medium businesses. A natural bolt-on for keg system referrals.
- **Host-a-tasting.** An existing owner hosts a brew tasting for around **20**
  friends, with a kickback or free gear for anything sold. Referral mechanics,
  run by the customer.
- **Jim's Mowing** was raised as another possible partner, on the strength of
  their presence in the outdoor and backyard space. A passing mention only.
- **Shipping kegs for refill is solvable.** Wine, meat and oysters all ship
  temperature-controlled.
