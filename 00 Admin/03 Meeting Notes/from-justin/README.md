- **Solution:** Catching up with friends made easy. Pub-quality beer and non-beer products on tap at home. **2.5 cases for the price of 1, or $2.55/schooner.** Perfect addition to your outdoor entertaining area, be the envy of your friends. GOAL = bringing people together.

- **Team:** production, marketing, bar, wholesale, outsourcing.
