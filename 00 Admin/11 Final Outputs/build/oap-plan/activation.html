The destination programme

The room already makes money on events. This is about selling them deliberately.

Tours. Get onto the circuit. The bus parking is an asset, not a liability. Build the Urban Legends booking widget so operators stop coordinating venue by venue.

Weddings and functions. PR to tourism operators, event and wedding planners. The signature wedding brew turns one booking into reception volume and a take-home sample.

Local business and ticketed music. Justin's own taproom priorities: lunch and after-work trade from local business, and ticketed music events.

Competitions, two a year. Each keg system giveaway moves about 30 systems. Justin's call is two a year, not three. The insider offer now lands the moment someone enters, and the Crafted Festival funnel (free taste and paddle, entry, automation) is the template to repeat.

Party rental is the quiet one. A system in a house for a weekend is a demo the host runs for you, in front of the people who might buy one.

First dated event: Oktoberfest plus the keg system giveaway. Taproom membership sits on Justin's three-month list; the business sheet flags two membership programmes as a cost to resolve.
