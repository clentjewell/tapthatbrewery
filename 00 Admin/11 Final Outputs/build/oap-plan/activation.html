The destination programme

The room already makes money on events. This is about selling them deliberately.

Tours. Get onto the circuit. The bus parking is an asset, not a liability. Build the Urban Legends booking widget so operators stop coordinating venue by venue.

Weddings and functions. PR to tourism operators, event and wedding planners. The signature wedding brew turns one booking into reception volume and a take-home sample for every guest.

Ticketed events and collabs. Own the calendar rather than waiting for walk-ins.

Party rental is the quiet one. A system in a house for a weekend is a demo that the host runs for you, in front of exactly the people who might buy one.

Romance the Crafted Brewers' Choice award everywhere in this. It is the reason a tour operator picks this stop.
