The north star

1,000 keg systems in the market. Every decision gets tested against whether it moves that number.

The shape decision underneath it: refills lead, and the Tap Room stops being run as a walk-in venue. It becomes a destination that sells events, tours and functions. The two halves have been cancelling each other out, because a good refill business takes traffic out of the room.

If nothing is decided, the default continues. Marketing time keeps going to the half that loses money, the best insights stay buried in a long document, and 1,000 systems stays a number on a slide.

Why this exists. Christy's reading of the Discovery set was that everything in it is equal, so none of it has shape: everything is important, the best insights are buried inside other insights, and nothing has been stress-tested. Clent asked for three things off the back of it, and they are the test for this page. Belief, because the work is already good. Clarity, on which few things get picked up. Momentum, because a decision made is worth more than a strategy admired.

Four plans, one move each : the rest of this pack is a page per plan

Business

Stop funding the losing half.

Pause walk-in trading and redirect that spend into refills and wholesale. Resource wholesale properly, because one account is worth about ten households and it is currently falling through the cracks.

First step: Justin confirms the walk-in call, and wholesale gets a person with a target.

Brand

Own the category, not the price.

The enemy is the overpriced pub, not the operator across the road. Cut the tail of about 19 brews to a set of heroes, put the Crafted award everywhere, and start designing for her.

First step: the award goes on the site, the room and the decals this month.

Sales and marketing

Connect the system before spending more.

Website, POS and CRM do not talk to each other, so every campaign underperforms. Then convert the owners who already bought elsewhere and take the friction out of reordering.

First step: connect the three systems, then replace the 90-day SMS.

Activation

Sell the room as a destination.

Tours, weddings, functions and ticketed events, with the artwork and the booking path already built. Partnerships where the systems are already being sold.

First step: get on the tour circuit and open the Harvey Norman conversation.

Sequence
Now

Justin picks the moves he is committing to. Connect the data. Pause walk-in spend.

Next

Convert existing owners. Fix reorder friction. Stand wholesale up as a role with targets.

Later

Range rationalisation, the wellness line, designing for her, and the duopoly workaround.

Nothing in Later starts until the Now column is done. That is the discipline the plan is buying.
