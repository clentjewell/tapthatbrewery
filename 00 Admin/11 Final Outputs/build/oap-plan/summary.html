The north star

1,000 active keg refillers by year three, across the Gold Coast, Brisbane and Northern Rivers, residential and commercial, with 250 at six months (March 2027). Every decision gets tested against whether it moves that number.

The shape decision underneath it: settled in principle on 18 September. The taproom is an events and local business venue, not a walk-in bar. Justin's own words: Oktoberfest is a sugar hit, and he would take week-on-week revenue over it. What is still open is whether the room reads as a showroom or an event space, and that is Justin's call inside 30 days.

If nothing is decided, the default continues. Marketing time gets split across three shapes at once, the best insights stay buried in a long document, and 1,000 refillers stays a number on a page.

Why this exists. Christy's reading of the Discovery set: everything in it is equal, so none of it has shape. Clent asked for three things off the back of it: belief, clarity, momentum, because a decision made beats a strategy admired.

North star is Justin's, from his plan on a page. JP framed it in the catch-up as 1,000 systems; refillers is the better measure, since a system not refilling is worth nothing.

Business

Settle the shape, then the split.

Justin's own 30-day call: showroom or event space. Walk-ins are not the strategy. Alongside it, clarity on who is buying, residential vs commercial, and where the energy goes.

First step: Justin picks the shape and the residential-vs-commercial split, inside 30 days.

Brand

Home entertainment, not beer.

Justin's line, every household deserves beer on tap, is the ambition, not the brand idea. The brand idea is that Tap That turns an ordinary catch-up into the best one on the street. The kegerator belongs beside the outdoor kitchen and the high-end barbecue, not beside the bar fridge.

First step: rewrite key messaging for each target market on that basis, inside 30 days, Christy to lead.

Sales and marketing

Switch the owners who already have a system.

Justin's number one priority: find every existing keg system owner and move them onto Tap That refills. Alongside it, the website upgrade plus SEM, and a referral programme with JVPs.

First step: build the outreach list of existing system owners and start the switch.

Activation

Oktoberfest, then the room as a destination.

A successful Oktoberfest and keg system giveaway first. Then taproom traffic from local business, functions and ticketed music events, the agreed priority for the room, not walk-ins.

First step: deliver Oktoberfest and the giveaway, then book the first ticketed music night.
