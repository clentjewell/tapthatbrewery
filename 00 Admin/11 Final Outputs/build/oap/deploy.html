Risks & Consent Gates
  • Advertising standards – a member-only price claim is running publicly right now. Blocking on every runbook that touches creative.
  • ABAC – 18+ targeting enforced at ad-set level; no consumption-rate or health claims; Benchy content shows the vehicle parked.
  • Consent – per channel, per contact, under the Spam Act. Giveaway entrants may hold promotion-only consent; those are suppressed, not mailed.
  • Delivery – no creative until the service is licensed. 51% want it; that is demand, not permission.
  • Capacity – one marketer. Two heavy builds in a month is the failure mode; windows move rather than stack.
  • Integration – no GoTab × Fishbowl date. Every automation ships with a manual path or it doesn’t ship.
