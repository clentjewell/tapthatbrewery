Evidence note (19 Aug 2026). Figures in this draft predate the client's census and planning documents. Several have been corrected; some interpretations are still under revision. Read 20A Evidence Reconciliation before citing anything here.

Pre-CP1 draft — produced ahead of Discover sign-off; census data, membership pricing reconciliation and competitor verification may revise inputs.

The job

Launch paid social (Meta-first) to feed the two funnels — switcher refills and system nurture — but do not scale spend until #57 EDM and #58 CRM are live: paying to fill a funnel that still leaks at 75–90 days wastes the budget the business doesn't have. Launch = test budgets; scale = gated on the critical path (#61). (Source plan: #46 Paid Media Plan not yet drafted — this brief works from the Discover base and H1/H2/H3; reconcile when #46 lands.)

Campaign structure

Campaign Objective Audience Landing
1. Switcher ("We Fill Any System") Conversions — first fill / enquiry GC radius; homebrew/kegerator/keg interests; corny-keg lookalikes from customer list We Fill Any System page
2. Giveaway (2×/yr bursts) Lead gen — entries Broad GC 25–65, segment-split creative Giveaway capture page
3. System nurture / retargeting Conversions — demo booked Site visitors, calculator users, giveaway entrants, video viewers; 90-day windows matched to the 3-month cycle Keg systems page
4. Benchy Conversions / partner traffic 4WD, camping, caravan, boating interests QLD/northern NSW Benchy page

Customer-list custom audiences (206 owners / 96–113 active + churned) are excluded from acquisition and served only lifecycle offers if #57/#58 gaps require paid backup.

H2 is a live test: kudos/connection-led vs cost-led per segment, called at the kill-criteria review.

Budget

[TBC with client]. Proposed shape until then: 60% switcher + retargeting (cheapest actives), 25% giveaway bursts, 15% Benchy test. No scale-up past test budgets before the gate above.

Age-gating & compliance

  • Meta alcohol rules: 18+ targeting minimum AU, age-gated landing pages, no minors in alcohol creative (#54 releases).
  • ABAC: no excess cues (census moderation stat is the tone-setter), no implied social/sexual success from drinking, no under-25-looking talent. RTD/seltzer creative follows the same rules.
  • Gluten-reduced wording only; no named competitor references (spelling unverified anyway).

Tracking setup

  • Meta Pixel + Conversions API on #53 events: demo_booked, giveaway_entry, calculator_used, first_fill (via GoTab/CRM feedback, #58).
  • UTM schema shared with GA4; weekly dashboard: cost per giveaway entry, cost per demo, cost per first fill, cost per new active (the only number that ultimately matters — feeds #19 KR 1.1's 7–8 switchers/month).

Kill criteria (reviewed fortnightly)

  1. Any ad set > 2× the campaign's average cost-per-result after $[TBC] spend → off.
  2. Switcher campaign not producing first fills ≤ $[TBC] CPA within 6 weeks → creative rebuild before more spend.
  3. Retargeting frequency > 4/week without conversion lift → cap or refresh.
  4. Any compliance flag or comment-thread trouble on alcohol creative → pause immediately, review before relaunch.

Success criteria

Launch live with all four campaigns, tracking verified end-to-end (test conversions reach the dashboard), and the first H2 creative test structured for a clean read within one buying cycle.

Assumptions & open items

  • Budget, offer economics and CPA thresholds [TBC]; ad account/pixel access and history unaudited.
  • Scale gate depends on #57/#58 delivery dates.
  • Google Ads (search) deliberately phase 2 — organic (#55) claims the clusters first; revisit at scale gate.
