Strategic frame

SEO here is not traffic for its own sake — it serves the two acquisition doors from #37: catch the 3-month considerers while they research ("is a keg system worth it?"), and intercept the installed base at the moment of need ("kegerator refill near me"). Nobody on the Gold Coast owns the category in search any more than in market — the D06 white space applies verbatim. All volumes below are directional only [to validate — no keyword research run yet]; current rankings, indexation and authority are [pending site audit].

Capacity guardrail: one hybrid marketer at ~70% allocation. The plan above is roughly a dozen assets, built once and maintained — not a publishing treadmill. Sequence: refill/switcher terms first (highest intent, lowest volume needed), calculator second (link magnet + closer), Benchy third (uncontested), informational last.

Technical basics

  • Platform, CMS, page speed, mobile state all [pending site audit] — audit before any migration decision.
  • Clean crawlable URLs matching #38 (/keg-systems/2-tap, /we-fill-any-system, /keg-refills-gold-coast); 301s from any existing equivalents.
  • Schema: LocalBusiness/Brewery (hours, geo), Product (systems with at-cost pricing), FAQ markup on objection content, Event markup for music nights.
  • Title/meta pattern carries geography ("… | Tap That Brewery, Burleigh Heads") — the brand name is an asset and a pun; let it work.
  • GoTab ordering flows must not trap refill pages behind JavaScript-only rendering — confirm crawlability at build.
