## Purpose

This is the journey as it *actually* runs, built from discovery evidence: taproom conversion (20–30% [unverified – see #20B] of visitors buy a system), giveaway mechanics (2×/yr, ~1,000 entries, 50%-off close → 22–25 systems), the 3-month buying cycle (~60% take up to 3 months, 40% longer, ~10% within a month), and the 75/90-day lifecycle definitions already in use. Each stage records the customer’s state, the touchpoints that exist today, the gap, and who owns it. #29 supplies the words per stage; #30 designs the pathway that closes the gaps.

## The journey on a page

Unaware → **Aware** (taproom visit / giveaway entry) → **Consideration** (the 3-month window) → **Purchase** (50%-off close, Afterpay/Zip, lease-to-buy pending) → **Activation** (first refill) → **Habit** (~1.55 kegs/month (census), Keg Crew) → **Advocacy** (referrals, UGC) → **At-risk** (75–90 days) → **Churned / Winback** (90+ days).

The structural finding, carried from D05/D06: the physical journey works – the digital journey barely exists. In venue there is a demo floor, a price board and a giveaway form; online there is memory. The 3-month consideration window, where 60% of buyers live, is currently unmanaged.

## Three journey-level findings

1. **The journey has a front door and a back door but no hallway.** Stages 2 and 4 work (taproom converts, giveaway closes); stage 3 – where 60% of buyers spend up to 3 months – is empty. Every improvement in #29/#30 concentrates there first.
2. **Activation is treated as purchase’s afterthought, but it is the real conversion.** Revenue starts at the first refill, not the sale ($975 carries no margin). The purchase moment must hand over three things by default: membership, a booked/prompted first refill, and the partner’s tap list.
3. **The lifecycle is defined but not instrumented.** 75/90-day definitions exist and a winback runs monthly by hand. Until GoTab/Fishbowl lands, the at-risk stage (the cheapest save) has no touchpoint at all – a named gap with a monthly revenue cost.

## Assumptions & open items

- Stage conversion figures (20–30% taproom, 22–25 per giveaway close) are client-reported; instrument them as baseline metrics in #30 rather than treating them as verified constants.
- Lease-to-buy timing ("couple of months", GoTab-dependent) and the Uber Direct delivery integration are pending – both alter stage 4 and 5 design when live.
- The at-risk/churn reason split (moderating vs drifted vs defected) is anecdotal from founder phone calls; validate once CRM data flows.
