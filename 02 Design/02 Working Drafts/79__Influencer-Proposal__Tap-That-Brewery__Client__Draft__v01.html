Why creators, why now

Get found by people choosing where to go this weekend

We want to put Tap That in front of Gold Coast and Northern NSW locals as they decide where to go this weekend. The ones who love the visit then get a reason to think about the best night in, on tap. The taproom is your demo floor, so every good visit is the start of a keg system sale.

Tap That's Instagram has about 2K followers. The local food and what's-on creators in this plan each reach 5K to 80K Gold Coast locals. We lead with micro creators, with 5K to 40K followers in your own suburbs. They usually get stronger engagement and more trust than big accounts, and they're happy to trade content for a great hosted experience. We only pay creators who have shown their audience actually responds.

A ready-made opening. Inside Gold Coast's September brewery checklist lists 20 Gold Coast breweries, and Urban List's "16 of the best" lists 16. Tap That isn't on either. Those are the first two emails we'd send. Must Do Gold Coast's brewery guide already includes Tap That, so that's a warm contact to build on.
  • Food, venue and what's-on creators do most of the work. We found no active craft beer creator on the Gold Coast. A small home-and-outdoor group carries the keg system and the Benchy portable tap.
  • Instagram first. Many of the best local creators post mainly on TikTok, but TikTok tightened its rules on alcohol content from 31 August 2026. Their collaborations run on Instagram. Tap That has no TikTok account yet, and TikTok's rules don't allow a creator to post a gifted or paid alcohol collaboration there without the brand's approval, so we don't ask for TikToks.
  • Every creator is checked before we contact them. We confirm live follower numbers, look back over six months of posts, and check their age and their audience's age.

How to work with them

Gifted first, paid when it's earned

We start every creator with a hosted visit, except media pages and managed talent, who only work paid. Money goes only to creators whose audiences have responded. Every paid deal includes usage rights, so you can boost the best Reels to 25–55-year-olds within 20 km of Burleigh.

Suggested 90-day budget: $2,000–$3,000

Most of the programme runs on hospitality, not cash. Editorial pitches and event listings cost nothing. Paid work is limited to two pieces until the gifted visits have shown what works.

~$700hosted visits: 6–8 visits, product at retail value
~$500creator night: product and pizza
$800–1,500two paid pieces: one what's-on feature and one creator follow-up
$0–250ABAC pre-vetting, only if a paid piece needs it
Optional step-up, decided at week 6: $2,000–$4,000. This covers the bigger pieces: Jon Wyeth's home keg system video, Mick's paid Benchy video and a Must Do Gold Coast review. We only recommend it if the first six weeks clear a simple gate: gifted Reels averaging 3%+ engagement per view, and 3+ keg system enquiries or 20+ code mentions at the bar by week 6. If the gate isn't cleared, we don't spend it.

For scale: a new keg refill customer is worth about $2,400 a year in refills, so one or two new keg customers pays back the core programme on revenue. Product is counted at retail value, so its cost to Tap That is lower. Cash to creators and publishers is paid directly by you, separately from Jewell fees. We get a quote before anything is booked, and nothing paid goes ahead without your approval.

Keeping you compliant

Australian alcohol marketing rules

Because Tap That promotes alcohol, every creator post has to meet Australian alcohol marketing rules. Tap That is responsible for any content it pays for or gifts product for, and for anything it reposts. That applies whether or not Tap That has signed up to ABAC, because ABAC also rules on complaints about non-signatories and publishes its decisions. We build these rules into every agreement, brief and approval.

Every creator also signs a one-page agreement covering deliverables, disclosure, age-gating, comment moderation, takedown on request, usage rights and ABAC compliance, and does ABAC's free one-hour online course. This is a working summary, not legal advice. Confirm the TikTok and Meta settings on the platforms before launch.

What makes it measurable

    
    
      
  • Instagram Collab posts. Every creator Reel is posted as a Collab with @tapthatbreweryandkeghouse, so it shows on Tap That's profile too. Tap That then sees its views and new followers directly.
    • 
      
  • Day-7 screenshots. Every creator agreement asks for reach, plays, saves and shares seven days after posting. There's no other way to see a creator's own numbers.
    • 
      
  • Two small setup jobs in week 1. A "creator code" button in GoTab so bar staff can log a mention in one tap, and "How did you hear about us?" on the keg demo form.
    • 
      
  • Keg system sales tracked for six months. Anyone who enquires through a creator is followed for six months, not just 90 days, so later purchases still count.

At day 90 we recommend the 3–5 creators to keep for six months.
