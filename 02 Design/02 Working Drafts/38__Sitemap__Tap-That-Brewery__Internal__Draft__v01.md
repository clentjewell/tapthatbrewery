## Structure logic

Two front doors, one engine. Door one is the **consideration journey** (Keg Systems and its supporting objection content) for the 90%+ who take a month to three-plus to buy. Door two is the **switcher shortcut** (We Fill Any System) for the installed base that skips consideration entirely. Everything else – taproom, story, memberships – exists to feed, de-risk or retain those two. Current site structure is [pending site audit]; this sitemap is the proposal to migrate toward, not a diff against what exists.

## Navigation and footer

- **Primary nav (max 6):** Keg Systems · Refills & Beer Range · We Fill Any System · Taproom · Memberships · Book a Demo (button).
- Benchy, About, FAQ, Events, Functions sit under their hubs or the footer – reachable, not competing for nav weight.
- **Persistent elements:** giveaway/Brew Buds capture (site-wide banner or footer block), Afterpay/Zip badges near any price, Instagram pull-through (@tapthatbreweryandkeghouse).
- **Deliberately excluded for now:** online hardware checkout (the demo and giveaway close better than a cold $975 cart – revisit with lease-to-buy), a general blog (objection content lives under FAQ/Systems until there’s capacity to sustain more; one hybrid marketer at ~70% cannot feed a content mill).

## Assumptions & open items

- Existing pages, URLs and redirect needs [pending site audit] – this sitemap may map onto current pages rather than require rebuilds.
- Whether GoTab supports web refill ordering/reservation determines if "Order a refill" is transactional or a booking form – confirm before build.
- Configurator vs static system pages: recommend static pages first (cheaper, SEO-ready), configurator later if data supports it.
- Membership page blocked on price reconciliation; giveaway page timing depends on next competition date and QLD permit requirements.
