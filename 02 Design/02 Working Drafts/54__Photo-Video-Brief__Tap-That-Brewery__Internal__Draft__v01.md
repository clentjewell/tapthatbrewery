## The job

Build the asset library that #52 (collateral), #53 (website), #56 (paid) and #59 (PR) all draw on. The brand rule is absolute: **no stock-photo gloss**. Discovery named UGC from real customers (not the owners on camera) as identified-but-underexecuted; this shoot makes it systematic. Everything should look like it was shot at a mate’s place, because it was – real customers, their systems, their backyards. Phone-native 9:16 video is a first-class deliverable, not an afterthought.

## Shot lists per segment (customer shoots)

Recruit from the 206 owners / 96–113 active (CRM pull, #58; incentive: refill credit [TBC]). One half-day per household, 4–6 households minimum.

| Segment | Setting | Must-get shots |
|---|---|---|
| **Young families** | Backyard, arvo light | The pour mid-BBQ; kids in frame doing kid things (never near the taps); partners with seltzer/Strawberry Gin Fizz in hand; the "nobody’s driving home" wide of the whole yard |
| **Cashed-up tradies** | Shed / man-cave install | The multi-tap wall as the crown of the build; the mates'-reaction moment; install detail (lines, fonts, decals); ute + Benchy at the campsite/4WD setup if a Benchy unit is placeable |
| **Boomers** | Family lunch/BBQ | Three generations, host pouring generously; the corny-keg drop-off ritual; quality cues (glassware, the $14 Tap That glass) |
| **Partner-drinks overlay** | Woven through all above | Her drink poured from the same wall – the "a third of the taps are hers" beat; genuine, never staged "wife approves" cheese |

Every household: a 30–60s phone-style testimonial ("what do you actually pay a month / what did your mates say / what does she drink off it").

## Venue & brewhouse

- Bar rail with working brewhouse behind (fermenters in frame – the freshness proof).
- Tap wall left + right (27 taps, non-beer third visible); coolroom "Kegged & Ready" board; Tap Token leaderboard; shuffleboard and mezzanine atmosphere on a busy night (event night ideal – pair with #60).
- The demo moment: staff walking a punter through a system – the 20–30% [unverified – see #20B] conversion, on camera.

## Product

- Every system SKU (2/3/4/6-tap, integrated, 5L mini) clean on white/neutral AND in-situ; plastic keg vs corny keg comparison; the Benchy in outdoor use (hero priority – no competitor imagery exists anywhere in the category).
- Pour details: head, condensation, Midnight in Tokyo black pour with award context.

## Founder portraits

Chris Smith (at the brewhouse, working – the ex-Burleigh-Barrels comeback story needs a face) and Justin Mistry (in the venue); one relaxed dual portrait. Editorial-usable resolution for #59 PR. Founders are **not** the faces of UGC content – portraits are for press and About page only.

## Usage rights

- Written model release per customer household covering paid social, web, print, PR, in perpetuity, all channels; children’s imagery released by parents and excluded from paid alcohol advertising.
- No visible faces of anyone unreleased; venue crowd shots signed off via event-night notice + targeted releases for featured patrons.

## Success criteria

1. Library tagged by segment × pillar × format, minimum: 40 finished stills, 12 × 9:16 cuts (15/30s), 4 testimonials, full product set, 2 founder portraits.
2. Zero frames that could pass for stock; every hero image contains a real Tap That Brewery system or tap wall.
3. All releases filed before any asset ships to #56.
