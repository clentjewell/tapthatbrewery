## The job, in one line

Put Tap That in front of Gold Coast and Northern NSW locals deciding where to go this weekend, and give the ones who love the visit a reason to think about **the best night in, on tap**. Visits first, keg systems second: the taproom is the demo floor.

**Why micro creators and local media.** Tap That's Instagram has about 2K followers. The local what's-on pages on this list reach 33K–84K Gold Coast locals each, and the micro creators reach people in Tap That's own suburbs. Hosted visits and free editorial pitches do most of the work; money goes only to creators whose audiences respond.

**Three ready-made openings.** Inside Gold Coast's September brewery checklist (20 breweries) and Urban List's "16 of the Gold Coast's best breweries" both leave Tap That off. Must Do Gold Coast's brewery guide already includes Tap That.

## What the research found (checked 29 September)

- **27 candidates researched, 21 kept.** Every count was re-checked from the platforms' own data. Six were dropped (see below).
- **No active Gold Coast craft beer creator exists.** The two we found are dormant or near-zero engagement. Food, venue and what's-on creators carry the programme.
- **Search and third-party stats were unreliable.** @gc_eats showed 14% engagement on a stats site; measured, it is ~0.55%. What's On Goldy showed 33.5K; it has 67K. Use only the live figures below.
- **The big numbers are on TikTok.** Ethan's median is 25K views, but on TikTok. Our picks' Instagram posts typically get 30–120 likes. Targets are set accordingly.
- **Instagram only.** Tap That has no TikTok account. Since 31 August 2026 a gifted or paid alcohol post on TikTok needs the brand's approval through TikTok One, so we never ask creators for TikToks.
- **Several creators need a tight brief.** Ethan's brewery review jokes about getting "smashed"; Lauren's Burleigh bar hits are bottomless brunches; Lucy has used "$5 margs" framing. All fixable with the brief and draft approval, but they are flagged.
- **No creator's age is publicly confirmed.** Confirm 25+ for every creator before booking.

## How Tap That should work with them

| Model | Who it's for | What Tap That gives | What Tap That gets | Indicative cost |

| **1. Hosted brewery experience** | Micro creators | Creator + one adult guest: brewer-led tour with Chris, tasting paddle, Ammazza pizza, a Tap That glass | 1 Instagram Reel (Collab post) + 3–5 Stories, paid partnership label, #ad, 18+ | ~$80–120 per visit at retail |
| **2. Food + drinks for content** | Inbound requests, wave 2 | Pizza and a paddle, no tour | Stories on the night; a Reel if they choose | ~$40–60 |
| **3. Paid collaboration** | Media pages, managed talent, top performers | Fee + hosted visit; a keg system or Benchy on loan for home and outdoor | Agreed deliverables, 30–90 days paid usage, day-7 insights | Micro $150–500 per Reel; media feature by quote; 90K+ creator $1,500–4,000 |
| **4. Creator night** | 8–12 creators + guests | A private hour before a live-music night: new-release tasting with Chris, pizza, then the gig | A weekend burst of Stories and Reels | ~$500 |
| **5. Longer-term relationship** | Best 3–5 after day 90 | Six months: monthly visit, first taste of new beers, creator code, small retainer or keg system deal | Monthly content and a trusted local face | $300–800 per creator per month, or product |

**Rate evidence.** Australian agency guides (2026) put nano/micro creators (5–20K) at about $150–500 per Reel. Creators with 10K+ followers often expect a fee rather than a hosted visit alone. 90-day paid usage rights add 40–50% to a fee. None of the Gold Coast media pages publish prices, so every paid piece needs a quote. Creators without an ABN require 47% withholding on payments over $75; GST applies if they are registered.

**Why lean.** The business is loss-making and #46 Paid Media Plan sets a test-first rule. A keg refill customer is worth about $2,400 a year in refills, so one or two new keg customers pay back the core programme on revenue. Creator budget is paid directly by Tap That, separately from Jewell fees.

## Compliance: every creator, every post

Tap That is responsible for any creator content it pays for or gifts product for, and for anything it reposts, whether or not it is an ABAC signatory. The full checklist is in the tracker.

| Rule | In practice | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Creator is 25+**, looks it, not pregnant | Confirm before booking; no ages are public for our list | ABAC Code 3(b)(iii); Toolkit #3 |
| **Audience 80%+ adult** | Insights screenshot of audience age; drop family or teen-heavy accounts | ABAC Code 4(c); Guidance Notes |
| **Post restricted to 18+** | Instagram: Paid Partnership label → Audience restrictions → 18 | ABAC Code 4(b); Toolkit #3 |
| **Disclose clearly** | Paid Partnership label + #ad at the start. Gifted counts as paid | AANA Code of Ethics 2.7; ACCC |
| **Moderation is the story** | No rapid or excessive drinking, no skolling, games, dares or "getting smashed" jokes; no bottomless framing | ABAC Code 3(a) |
| **No success or mood claims** | The night is good because of the people, the music and the place | ABAC Code 3(c) |
| **No alcohol with vehicles, boats or water** | Benchy/4WD: vehicle parked, day done | ABAC Code 3(d) |
| **No free or cheap drink offers** | Creator codes are merch, food or keg system offers; never free beer or happy hour prices | Liquor Act 1992 (Qld) s142ZZC |
| **Tap That approves before posting** | Every draft; paid pieces over $1K or anything borderline to ABAC pre-vetting ($250/half hour) | ABAC Toolkit #2; Pre-vetting Service |
| **Instagram only, no TikToks** | Gifted/paid alcohol content on TikTok needs the brand's approval via TikTok One (policy effective 31 Aug 2026). Tap That has no TikTok account | TikTok Branded Content Policy |
| **Giveaways with a liquor prize** | Free entry, 18+, date-of-birth check | Charitable and Non-Profit Gaming Act 1999 (Qld) s110 |
| **Words** | "Gluten-reduced", never "gluten-free"; don't lead with ABV; *Enjoy responsibly. 18+.* | #41 Social Strategy |

## Content brief (one page, sent to every creator)

- **Who we are:** An independent brewery and taproom in Burleigh Heads. We brew on site, pour 27 taps (a third are seltzers, sours and cocktails), host live music and tours, and put beer on tap in people's homes. *The best night in, on tap.*
- **The visit:** [date/time]. You + 1 guest, both 18+. Tour with Chris, a tasting paddle, pizza, first taste of [new release].
- **Deliverables:** 1 Instagram Reel (20–45s) + 3–5 Instagram Stories within 7 days. Not on TikTok.
- **Must include:** Post the Reel as an Instagram Collab with @tapthatbreweryandkeghouse and tag the location. Paid Partnership label. **#ad** at the start. **Audience restriction 18+**. *Enjoy responsibly. 18+.*
- **Great shots:** Walking in past the brewhouse · the tap wall · the paddle landing · Chris pouring or explaining a beer · the Kegged & Ready board · your honest first sip and verdict · the room and the music.
- **Talking points (in your words):** brewed right here · something on tap for everyone · fresh beer · you can have it on tap at home.
- **Please don't:** show anyone under 25 drinking · skol, race or play drinking games · show more than a couple of drinks each · joke about getting drunk · talk up the beer's strength · say beer makes you relax, happy or the life of the party · show drinking near cars, boats or the pool · mention free drinks, discounts, bottomless or happy hour prices · say "gluten-free" · post this collaboration on TikTok.
- **Approval:** Send us the draft before posting. We aim to reply within 24 hours.
- **Results:** Seven days after posting, send a screenshot of the Reel's insights: reach, plays, likes, comments, saves and shares.
- **Usage:** Tap That may repost and share. [Paid: may boost as an ad for 30/60/90 days.]
- **Your code:** [CREATORNAME] gets your followers [a free Tap That glass on their visit / $50 off keg system delivery]. We'll track mentions at the bar.
