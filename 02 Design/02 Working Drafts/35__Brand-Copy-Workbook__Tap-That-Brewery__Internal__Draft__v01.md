Working kit for anyone writing in the Tap That Brewery voice. Rules: cost never opens (except high-volume lane), puns for moments and names, plain talk for money and service, never binge, never dad-only.

## 1. Tagline / headline bank

### By pillar

**Connection**
- Good times on tap at your place. *(roof line)*
- The house with the taps is the house everyone ends up at.
- Your backyard. Everyone's local.

**Kudos**
- Nice setup. Say it again.
- The pour that makes the shed.
- From esky to envy.

**Convenience**
- Grab a keg. Go. (No CO2, no fuss, no warm slabs.)
- In by Tuesday knock-off, ready Friday.
- The bottle-o run, retired.

**Taste/Freshness**
- Brewed in Burleigh. Poured at yours.
- 20 litres at a time, so it never sits around.
- Award-winning in year one. (Midnight in Tokyo — Brewer's Choice, Crafted.)

**Cost (closing contexts only)**
- Cheaper than a night out. Nobody needs a babysitter.
- 44 schooners a keg. You do the maths — we already did.
- The system at cost. We're in the good-times business, not the hardware business.

## 2. Objection-handling copy blocks

**"$975 is a lot."**
> It's the same price we pay for it — the system's at cost, because we make our living on refills and good times, not hardware. Afterpay and Zip split it; the giveaway happens twice a year if you'd rather win one; and every system comes with keg credit to get you pouring.

**"My wife won't let me."**
> Fair. Show her the tap list — a third of our 27 taps aren't beer. Strawberry Gin Fizz, seltzers, ginger beer, mojitos on tap. This isn't your keg system, it's the house's.

**"Nowhere to put it."**
> The 2-tap fits where a bar fridge fits. Start with the 5L mini system ($225) if you want proof before you commit a corner of the garage.

**"I'll drink too much."**
> We asked 50 of our keg owners: 70% pour daily or most days — and still only go through one or two kegs a month. It's not more beer, it's better beer. (And yes, one bloke locks his.)

**"The place across the road is cheaper."** *(internal guidance — never raise it first)*
> Never argue the sticker. Answer: what's on their other 26 taps? Range, freshness, the award, Keg Crew, tokens, the birthday keg. Then stop talking.

## 3. Boilerplate

**Short (25 words):**
> Tap That Brewery, Burleigh Heads: award-winning beer on tap at home. We brew it, keg it in 20s, and fill any system.

**Medium (60 words):**
> Tap That Brewery is the Gold Coast's home-tap brand. We brew award-winning beer in Burleigh (Midnight in Tokyo — Brewer's Choice at Crafted), keg it fresh in 20-litre kegs, and fill any keg system — whoever sold it to you. 27 taps in the taproom, a third of them not even beer. Good times at your place beat a night out anywhere.

**Long (100 words):**
> Tap That Brewery is a Burleigh Heads brewery in the business of connection and good times — keg systems and refills are just the means. Founded by head brewer Chris Smith and operator Justin Mistry, Tap That Brewery won Brewer's Choice at Crafted (Australia's biggest beer festival) in its first year with Midnight in Tokyo, a black Japanese lager. The model is simple: keg systems at cost, fresh 20-litre refills brewed on site, and 27 taps — a third of them seltzers, cocktails and RTDs — so there's a pour for everyone. Any keg system filled, whoever sold it. Your place, everyone's local.

## 4. Key phrases to own

- **"Beer on tap at home"** — the category (already on the in-venue flyer)
- **"We fill any system"** — the flank (H1)
- **"Good times on tap at your place"** — the roof line
- **"Brewed in Burleigh"** — the freshness warrant
- **"The system at cost"** — the honesty offer
- **"Knockoffs"** — Tap That Brewery's word for happy hour / end of the working day; keep using it
- Existing owned names to keep: **Keg Crew, Tap Tokens, Bring the Pub to You, Tap That Knockoffs**

## 5. Banned phrases and clichés

Craft-washing: "crafted with passion", "artisanal", "small-batch perfection", "hoppy goodness", "for true beer lovers". Discount-shouting: "CHEAP KEGS", "LOWEST PRICES", "%-off" in openers. Binge cues: "get smashed", "endless beer", "never run dry", "drink up". Corporate beige: "premium beverage solutions", "your one-stop shop", "look no further". Anything crude beyond the wink of the name itself.

## 6. Voice dos & don'ts, with rewrites (flat → Tap That Brewery)

| Flat | Tap That Brewery |
|---|---|
| "Happy hour: discounted jugs 3–5pm weekdays." | "Tap That Knockoffs. Jugs from $16, Mon–Fri 3–5. You've earnt it." |
| "We offer a range of non-alcoholic and alternative beverage options." | "A third of our taps aren't even beer. Gin fizz, seltzers, ginger beer — her round's sorted." |
| "Purchase a keg system today from $975." | "The setup costs us $975. It costs you $975. We're in the refills business — the good times are the margin." |
| "Refer a friend and receive loyalty points." | "Mate buys a system, you bank 1,000 Tap Tokens. That's what mates are for." |
| "Our corny keg service has a fast turnaround." | "Corny in by Tuesday knock-off. Ready Friday. Easy." |
| "Support our small business by following us on social media." | "5 ways to support us. Total cost: $0.00." |
| "Our beer is fresh and high quality." | "Brewed in Burleigh, kegged in 20s, gone before it can go stale." |

**Dos:** write like you're leaning on the bar; use real numbers (44 schooners, 27 taps, $0.00); let the pun do one job then get out; say money things straight. **Don'ts:** stack puns; open with price; punch down; sound like a brewery brochure.
